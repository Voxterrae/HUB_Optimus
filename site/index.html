The method

Separate before interpreting.

The five stages are an operating discipline. Each stage limits what the next stage is allowed to claim.

  1. 01

    Reality

    What is directly observed.

  2. 02

    Evidence

    What supports or contradicts a claim.

  3. 03

    Inference

    What follows cautiously from the evidence.

  4. 04

    Narrative

    What may amplify, simplify, or distort.

  5. 05

    Operational Signal

    What is operationally relevant for human review.

Provider position

Portable by design.

GitHub provides versioning, review, CI, and the public static site. Analysis execution remains local or controlled and provider-independent. A hosting or model provider does not gain project or semantic authority.
