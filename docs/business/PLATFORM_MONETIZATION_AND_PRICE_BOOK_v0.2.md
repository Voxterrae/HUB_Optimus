## 1. Position

HUB_Optimus is not sold as a low-cost automation utility, a generic freelance
service, or a collection of disconnected scripts.

It is positioned as a future-facing technology and automation platform combining:

- founder-led enterprise architecture;
- applications and sector operating systems;
- governed connectors and control planes;
- APIs, SDKs, embedded interfaces, and iframe distribution;
- evidence and decision intelligence;
- secure orchestration and automation;
- auditability, observability, rollback, and policy enforcement;
- managed operation, support, and continuous evolution.

The market does not receive unlimited free access while HUB_Optimus proves its
value. Working systems, reproducible tests, governed pilots, controlled evidence,
and measurable results speak for the capability.

The commercial posture is:

> **HUB_Optimus does not chase organizations with unpaid bespoke work. Suitable
> organizations request access, pass qualification, demonstrate authority and
> budget, and fund the value they receive.**

This does not prohibit visibility, partnerships, public evidence, or selected
introductions. It prohibits speculative delivery, free custom proofs of concept,
and price competition that undervalues the platform.

## 2. How every commercial price is built

A complete HUB_Optimus commercial agreement can contain seven independent
components:

```text
1. Strategic diagnosis / implementation
2. Annual platform canon
3. Application and module licenses
4. Connector licenses
5. API / orchestration / AI usage
6. Embedded, iframe, SDK, white-label, or OEM rights
7. Support, SLA, environments, infrastructure, and compliance
```

A customer does not obtain all seven merely by paying for one. An implementation
fee does not replace the annual platform canon. A platform subscription does not
include unlimited custom development. An API entitlement does not grant
white-label, resale, source-code, or ownership rights.

## 4. Annual HUB_Optimus platform canon

The annual canon licenses the protected platform layer. It does not sell or
transfer the core.

| Platform level | Minimum annual canon |
| --- | ---: |
| HUB_Optimus Core Business | EUR 60,000/year |
| HUB_Optimus Enterprise | EUR 180,000/year |
| HUB_Optimus Strategic / Regulated / Public | EUR 360,000/year |
| HUB_Optimus Sovereign / OEM / Mission-Critical | EUR 750,000/year+ |

The canon normally covers the contracted core control plane, identity and tenant
boundary, administration, base telemetry, policy enforcement, and standard
support for the agreed scope. Applications, connectors, API usage, AI usage,
additional environments, implementation, dedicated infrastructure, premium
support, and distribution rights are priced separately unless the contract
bundles them explicitly.

### Term

- Minimum software term: 12 months.
- Default enterprise, strategic, and public-sector term: 36 months.
- Annual software is prepaid.
- Multi-year price protection requires a corresponding non-cancellable minimum
  commitment.
- Usage and pass-through charges may be invoiced monthly in arrears.

## 9. OEM, resale, and platform distribution

OEM and resale rights require a strategic agreement.

Default minimum:

```text
EUR 300,000 annual minimum guarantee
        OR
15% of net downstream software-license revenue
whichever is greater
```

OEM rights never automatically include:

- ownership of the HUB_Optimus core;
- unrestricted source code;
- perpetual rights;
- sublicensing outside the agreed channel;
- exclusivity;
- removal of security, governance, metering, or audit controls;
- authority to create free or discounted downstream licenses.

Exclusivity requires a defined sector or territory, limited term, minimum revenue,
performance duties, termination and reversion, and a separate premium.

## 12. Channel pricing

HUB_Optimus prices for the net value it must receive after channel costs.

- Direct enterprise contract: preferred for strategic projects.
- Microsoft Marketplace: price must preserve the target net amount after the
  marketplace fee; v0.2 uses a minimum 3.5% gross-up assumption.
- Approved Merchant of Record: price must preserve the target net amount after
  transaction, tax, billing, fraud, and support fees; v0.2 uses a minimum 6%
  gross-up assumption.
- Reseller or implementation partner: 20%–35% margin only when the partner funds
  and performs defined acquisition, contracting, implementation, first-line
  support, or minimum-revenue obligations.
- No double discount: a marketplace fee, reseller margin, multi-year discount,
  and strategic discount are not stacked without owner approval.

## 15. Quotation rule

No price in this document is an automatic offer. A valid quotation requires:

- identified legal entity and real beneficiary;
- scope, systems, users, data, environments, and jurisdictions;
- rights requested;
- implementation and support responsibility;
- security and compliance classification;
- commercial term and payment channel;
- owner-authorized proposal.

The bottom of a range is never the default. It assumes a narrow, low-risk,
well-prepared scope with reusable components and limited responsibility.
