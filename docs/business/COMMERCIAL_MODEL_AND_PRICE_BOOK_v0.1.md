# HUB_Optimus — Commercial Model and Price Book v0.1

**Status:** Initial owner pricing position proposed under issue `#1863`  
**Currency:** EUR  
**Taxes:** Prices exclude VAT and other applicable taxes  
**Purpose:** Establish a credible starting position; this is not an automatic quotation

## 1. Commercial model

HUB_Optimus is sold as a high-value combination of:

- founder-led architecture and strategic direction;
- decision intelligence and evidence governance;
- enterprise operating-system design;
- governed Microsoft 365, cloud, data, AI, and workflow integration;
- security, auditability, observability, and rollback design;
- implementation, managed operation, support, and continuous improvement;
- reusable platform capability licensed without surrendering the core.

The standard path is:

```text
Qualification
    ↓
Paid diagnostic and architecture
    ↓
Paid proof-of-value pilot
    ↓
Implementation
    ↓
License / managed operation / support
    ↓
Expansion, sector package, or strategic partnership
```

A customer may purchase only the stages it needs, but it does not receive later-
stage deliverables for free during an earlier stage.

### Minimum engagement

- SME or focused organization: **EUR 3,000**.
- Enterprise or public institution: **EUR 10,000**.
- An exception requires an explicit written owner decision.

### Separate quotation required

The following are never assumed to be included:

- source-code ownership or buyout;
- white-label rights;
- resale, sublicensing, or integration-partner rights;
- geographic, customer, or sector exclusivity;
- on-premises or isolated deployment;
- high-availability or 24/7 obligations;
- regulated-sector certification;
- model training using customer data;
- transfer of reusable connectors, methods, or platform governance;
- unlimited users, environments, mailboxes, data volume, or support;
- public tender guarantees, performance bonds, or unusual liability;
- joint-venture, equity, or revenue-sharing rights.

## 3. Pricing logic

A final price is based on:

- institutional value and risk reduced;
- number and complexity of systems and integrations;
- users, environments, data volume, and geographic scope;
- data sensitivity, security, audit, and regulatory requirements;
- delivery urgency and availability commitments;
- custom versus reusable work;
- customer dependencies and readiness;
- procurement and documentation burden;
- rights requested, exclusivity, source-code access, and transfer risk;
- support term and operational responsibility.

The lowest number in a range is not a default. It assumes a narrow, well-defined,
low-risk scope with prepared stakeholders, available data, and limited
integration complexity.

## 4. Payment model

### Diagnostics, audits, and workshops

- 100% payable before work begins when the engagement is EUR 10,000 or less.
- For larger diagnostic or audit engagements: 60% at booking and 40% before final
  delivery.

### Paid pilots

- 50% at reservation and kickoff;
- 30% at the agreed midpoint or design acceptance;
- 20% before production handover or final pilot report.

### Implementations above EUR 45,000

- 40% at reservation and kickoff;
- 30% at architecture/design acceptance;
- 20% at the main implementation milestone;
- 10% before final handover.

### Recurring services

- monthly or annual payment in advance;
- annual prepayment may justify a discount of up to 10%;
- usage, infrastructure, third-party licensing, and pass-through costs are
  separate unless explicitly included.

### General rules

- quotation validity: 15 calendar days unless stated otherwise;
- travel and approved expenses: billed at cost;
- work may pause when an invoice is overdue, subject to the signed contract;
- cancellation, acceptance, delay, and change-control rules must appear in the
  applicable agreement;
- procurement delays do not reserve capacity without an agreed payment or
  reservation fee.

## 5. No-free-work boundary

A free qualification call may explain public capability and confirm fit. It does
not include custom diagnosis, technical design, data review, prototype, code,
report, tender response, integration plan, workshop, or reusable deliverable.

For commercial and public-sector prospects:

- proofs of concept are paid;
- bespoke demonstrations are paid when they require custom preparation;
- tender or RFP work beyond standard public material is billable;
- unpaid discovery is capped at the qualification call;
- follow-up meetings involving architecture or solution design require an
  engagement;
- previous unpaid assistance does not create a right to future free work.

## 6. Discount policy

HUB_Optimus reduces scope before reducing value.

A discount must purchase measurable consideration, for example:

- advance or annual payment;
- a multi-year minimum commitment;
- lower support or liability obligations;
- permission for a carefully controlled case study;
- a reusable, nonexclusive implementation;
- committed distribution or minimum revenue;
- simplified procurement or customer-supplied resources.

The following are not consideration:

- exposure;
- prestige;
- the size or reputation of the organization;
- an unspecified future opportunity;
- access to senior decision-makers;
- an invitation to compete for later work;
- pressure created by a deadline.

Discounts above 15%, free pilots, or work below the minimum engagement require an
explicit written decision by Benjamin Gerrit Hoff.

## 7. Intellectual-property model

The default transaction is a license and professional-services relationship, not
a sale of the HUB_Optimus core.

- Customer data and customer pre-existing material remain the customer's.
- The HUB_Optimus platform, reusable architecture, methods, connectors, policies,
  templates, know-how, and generic improvements remain with Benjamin Gerrit
  Hoff / HUB_Optimus unless a separate signed agreement states otherwise.
- Customer-specific deliverables and usage rights are defined in the contract.
- Source-code transfer or ownership buyout is not offered by default and, if ever
  considered, must reflect replacement value, future opportunity cost, strategic
  risk, and a substantial premium.

## 8. Public institutions

Public institutions are paid clients by default.

Engagements should account for:

- procurement and tender documentation;
- transparency, accessibility, and auditability;
- data protection and public-record obligations;
- security and sovereignty requirements;
- acceptance, maintenance, support, and continuity duties;
- payment cycles, guarantees, and administrative burden.

A social mission does not authorize unpaid custom work. A direct humanitarian
emergency exception requires an explicit owner decision and a written scope.

## 9. Joint ventures and strategic distribution

The default structure is:

```text
funded discovery or pilot
        +
funded implementation
        +
optional equity / revenue share / distribution rights
        +
written governance and IP boundaries
```

Pure equity, speculative future revenue, or introductions do not normally pay
current delivery costs. Any exception requires Benjamin Gerrit Hoff's explicit
written approval.

Exclusivity requires:

- a defined sector or territory;
- a limited term;
- minimum commercial commitments;
- measurable performance obligations;
- termination and reversion rights;
- no ownership or control over the HUB_Optimus core.

## 10. Evidence-first commercial credibility

HUB_Optimus demonstrates value through:

- working systems;
- reproducible tests and benchmarks;
- governed pilots;
- audit trails and rollback;
- documented security and authority boundaries;
- redacted case studies where permitted;
- measurable before/after outcomes;
- references and controlled demonstrations.

It does not buy credibility by donating custom development to organizations that
can and should pay.
