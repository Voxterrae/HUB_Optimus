# HUB_Optimus — Commercial Value and Public-Benefit Charter

**Status:** Owner decision recorded in issue `#1863`; repository ratification proposed  
**Owner and final authority:** Benjamin Gerrit Hoff  
**Effective commercial currency:** EUR, excluding applicable taxes unless stated otherwise

## 1. Foundational economic philosophy

HUB_Optimus exists to create real capability, institutional value, better
decisions, safer systems, and measurable operational improvement.

Its work is not free merely because the platform is still being built or because
a large organization asks for a demonstration. Evidence, functioning systems,
reproducible tests, governed pilots, documented architecture, and delivered
results are the proof of capability.

The central rule is:

> **HUB_Optimus charges organizations for capability, value, risk reduction,
> implementation, knowledge, responsibility, and continued operation. It never
> charges for human or animal suffering.**

Commercial strength and solidarity are not opposites. Commercial revenue makes
independent public-benefit work sustainable.

## 2. Organizations that pay

The following are paid clients by default:

- companies and commercial groups;
- large corporations and their subsidiaries;
- professional firms and investment vehicles;
- public administrations, public institutions, public companies, agencies, and
  other bodies with public budgets;
- universities, research institutions, foundations, and associations when the
  engagement has an institutional budget, commercial beneficiary, procurement
  process, or funded deliverable;
- intermediaries, resellers, consultancies, integrators, and corporate social-
  responsibility programs acting for a commercial or institutional beneficiary.

Paid services include diagnosis, architecture, custom analysis, proof-of-value
pilots, implementation, integrations, licensing, managed operation, support,
training, audits, security and governance work, tenders, strategic advice, and
custom deliverables.

Public purpose does not automatically mean free work. Public institutions manage
public budgets and must contract professional capability transparently.

## 3. End of open-ended unpaid professional work

HUB_Optimus does not provide free bespoke professional work to organizations
that can pay.

A single introductory qualification conversation of up to 30 minutes may be
free. It may explain public capability and determine fit, but it includes no:

- tailored architecture;
- custom diagnosis or report;
- source code, integration, configuration, or implementation;
- bespoke proof of concept;
- procurement design;
- data analysis;
- deliverable that the organization can use internally or commercially.

A company or public institution may evaluate HUB_Optimus through public
materials, controlled demonstrations, reproducible evidence, redacted case
studies, and a paid pilot. Unpaid speculative building is not a prerequisite for
respect, recognition, or credibility.

Requests framed as exposure, prestige, future opportunity, access to decision-
makers, a potential later contract, or the size of the requesting organization
do not constitute payment.

## 4. HUB_Optimus Common Good

Qualifying work that directly advances human welfare, animal welfare,
humanitarian protection, essential rights, prevention of suffering, or another
approved common-good purpose may be provided through **HUB_Optimus Common Good**.

For an approved scope:

- the professional fee charged to the eligible beneficiary is zero;
- unavoidable third-party costs may be funded transparently at cost by a donor,
  grant, sponsor, or beneficiary, with no HUB_Optimus margin;
- the work is limited to the agreed humanitarian or public-benefit purpose;
- access is noncommercial, nontransferable, nonexclusive, and capacity-
  dependent;
- the HUB_Optimus core, methods, reusable components, source code, brand,
  governance, and ownership are not transferred;
- ongoing operation and support exist only when explicitly included in the pro
  bono scope.

Free to the beneficiary does not mean public-domain, unrestricted, or open-source
ownership. It means that HUB_Optimus does not profit from the approved public-
benefit service.

## 5. Absolute anti-exploitation boundary

HUB_Optimus Common Good must not generate profit from suffering, victims,
vulnerability, urgency, or donated resources.

The following are prohibited:

- selling, licensing, or reusing beneficiary or victim data for unrelated
  commercial purposes;
- using private stories, images, health information, distress, or vulnerability
  as marketing material without informed and revocable consent;
- taking a percentage of donations;
- charging a success fee based on the number of victims, emergencies, rescued
  persons or animals, suffering, illness, displacement, abuse, or harm;
- creating artificial dependency on HUB_Optimus;
- disguising a corporate, political, lobbying, reseller, surveillance, or
  extractive project as nonprofit assistance;
- granting a commercial third party free access through an NGO;
- using humanitarian access to train, profile, target, manipulate, or monitor
  vulnerable people or animals for unrelated purposes.

Publicity is never part of the price of receiving help. Any case study requires
separate, informed permission and must protect dignity and confidentiality.

## 6. Eligibility for the public-benefit lane

Eligibility requires a written assessment of:

- legal or mission-locked nonprofit status, or a clearly documented equivalent;
- direct benefit to humans, animals, or an approved common-good objective;
- absence of a hidden commercial beneficiary;
- legitimate governance and responsible data practices;
- realistic scope, safety, impact, and available capacity;
- noncommercial use of the resulting service;
- compliance with applicable law and safeguarding requirements.

Political parties, electoral campaigns, commercial resellers, lobbying vehicles,
corporate reputation projects, and entities using nonprofit status to obtain
free commercial development are not eligible.

A public institution remains a paid client by default. A direct humanitarian
emergency waiver may be granted only by an explicit written decision of Benjamin
Gerrit Hoff. Such a waiver is exceptional and creates no precedent.

## 7. Sustainable solidarity

Commercial activity finances the ability to help without becoming dependent on
the suffering of those receiving help.

The initial policy target is to reserve **5% of collected net commercial service
revenue, or equivalent delivery capacity**, for approved Common Good work once
commercial revenue begins. Benjamin Gerrit Hoff may increase that commitment.

The reserve:

- is not a marketing budget;
- is not exchanged for beneficiary publicity;
- cannot be diverted to free work for corporations or public institutions;
- should be reported separately once the contracting and accounting structure is
  operational.

## 8. Intellectual-property and data boundary

Standard commercial and pro bono engagements do not transfer ownership of the
HUB_Optimus core.

Unless a separate signed agreement expressly states otherwise:

- the client or beneficiary retains its own data and pre-existing materials;
- HUB_Optimus retains its platform, architecture, methods, reusable connectors,
  policies, templates, models, tools, know-how, improvements, and generic
  components;
- client-specific deliverables are licensed or assigned only as stated in the
  applicable contract;
- source code, exclusivity, white-label rights, resale rights, sector exclusivity,
  and ownership transfers are separate high-value negotiations;
- no engagement creates an implied joint venture, ownership share, agency, or
  governance right.

## 9. Joint ventures and strategic partners

A joint venture or strategic partnership requires a separate written agreement
approved by Benjamin Gerrit Hoff with fair, transparent, reciprocal, and clearly
scoped conditions.

The default economic rule is:

- current delivery is funded in cash or through an approved funded budget;
- equity, revenue share, distribution, or strategic access may supplement cash
  consideration but does not automatically replace it;
- no party receives the HUB_Optimus core merely by introducing a customer,
  providing infrastructure, participating in a pilot, or offering future reach;
- exclusivity requires a defined territory or sector, a limited term, measurable
  obligations, and an appropriate minimum guarantee.

## 10. Exceptions

Any exception to the paid-client rule, minimum engagement, public-benefit
eligibility, discount boundary, IP boundary, or joint-venture funding rule
requires an explicit written decision by Benjamin Gerrit Hoff.

Silence, urgency, a verbal promise, a chat message, an email from a third party,
a procurement deadline, or previous unpaid assistance does not constitute an
exception.

## 11. Contracting boundary

This Charter establishes business philosophy and internal governance. Each real
engagement still requires an appropriate proposal or agreement covering scope,
price, taxes, payment, data protection, confidentiality, security,
responsibilities, warranties, liability, intellectual property, support,
termination, and applicable law.

Public procurement, nonprofit qualification, state-aid rules, taxation,
invoicing, professional liability, and cross-border delivery require legal,
tax, and accounting review for the contracting entity and jurisdiction.

## Authoritative English summary

Companies, commercial organizations, and public institutions pay for bespoke
HUB_Optimus professional services. Free custom proofs of concept and open-ended
unpaid delivery are not the route to recognition. Qualifying human- and animal-
welfare nonprofit work may be delivered free to the beneficiary through
HUB_Optimus Common Good, without monetizing suffering, donations, vulnerability,
private stories, or beneficiary data. Public-benefit access does not transfer the
HUB_Optimus core or create unlimited support. Commercial revenue sustains the
solidarity lane.

## Verbindliche deutsche Kurzfassung

Unternehmen, kommerzielle Organisationen und öffentliche Institutionen bezahlen
individuelle professionelle Leistungen von HUB_Optimus. Kostenlose, speziell
entwickelte Proofs of Concept und unbegrenzte unentgeltliche Arbeit sind kein
Mittel, um Anerkennung zu erlangen. Qualifizierte gemeinnützige Projekte zum
Wohl von Menschen und Tieren können über **HUB_Optimus Common Good** für die
Begünstigten kostenfrei erbracht werden. Aus Leid, Spenden, Verletzlichkeit,
privaten Geschichten oder Daten der Begünstigten wird kein Gewinn erzielt. Die
kostenfreie gemeinnützige Nutzung überträgt weder den technologischen Kern von
HUB_Optimus noch unbegrenzte Supportansprüche. Kommerzielle Einnahmen sichern
die unabhängige Solidaritätsarbeit.
