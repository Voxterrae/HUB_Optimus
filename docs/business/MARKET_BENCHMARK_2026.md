# HUB_Optimus — Technology Monetization Market Benchmark 2026

**Research date:** 8 August 2026  
**Purpose:** Document public market anchors used to design v0.2  
**Important:** Public list prices are not directly comparable to a custom
HUB_Optimus enterprise program. They reveal market structure and minimum anchors,
not a single universal market price.

## 1. Market conclusion

The technology market consistently uses hybrid monetization:

```text
platform or edition fee
        +
users / apps / connectors / executions / credits
        +
usage or overage
        +
support and SLA
        +
implementation and professional services
        +
embedded / white-label / OEM rights
```

Enterprise integration, API management, embedded apps, and critical support are
not priced like commodity self-service automation.

## 3. Why HUB_Optimus v0.2 is above self-service tools

HUB_Optimus v0.2 is not merely the runtime cost of Zapier, n8n, Power Apps,
Retool, or an API gateway. Its commercial scope can include:

- architecture across multiple products and vendors;
- custom connectors and protected identities;
- decision and evidence governance;
- tenant, data, and security boundaries;
- customer-specific implementation and migration;
- observability, audit trails, rollback, and operational responsibility;
- embedded and downstream distribution rights;
- founder-led strategic accountability;
- enterprise support and regulated deployment.

The customer's cloud and third-party licenses remain additional costs. The
HUB_Optimus price represents the intellectual property, orchestration,
governance, implementation, distribution rights, and responsibility layered on
top.

## 4. Tax and payment operations evidence

- [Paddle explains](https://www.paddle.com/help/sell/tax/how-paddle-handles-vat-on-your-behalf)
  that, as Merchant of Record, it acts as seller, calculates and collects
  transaction tax, remits it, and issues compliant invoices in supported
  jurisdictions.
- [Lemon Squeezy states](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing) that its Merchant
  of Record service assumes sales-tax/VAT collection and filing responsibilities
  for eligible digital sales.
- [Microsoft Marketplace billing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/marketplace/billing-invoicing)
  can consolidate third-party software purchases into a customer's Microsoft
  billing relationship.
- Microsoft also warns that publisher tax duties vary by jurisdiction; a
  marketplace does not eliminate every publisher tax obligation.

Therefore v0.2 uses different channels for different transactions rather than
forcing HUB_Optimus to build a global tax stack from day one.

## 5. EU and Spain operational context

- The European Commission's [cross-border VAT guidance](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/taxation/vat/cross-border-vat/index_en.htm)
  explains that EU B2B services commonly use reverse charge when the customer is
  the liable business, while services to customers outside the EU are usually not
  charged EU VAT subject to exceptions.
- [VIES](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/taxation/vat/check-vat-number-vies/index_en.htm)
  is the official validation tool for cross-border EU VAT registration.
- The EU [late-payment guidance](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/finance-and-tax/making-receiving-payments/late-payment/index_en.htm)
  reports Spain's statutory commercial late-payment rate at 10.40% for
  1 July–31 December 2026 and a EUR 40 fixed recovery entitlement, subject to the
  applicable contract and law.
- Spain's 2026 tax-control plan records that VERI*FACTU adaptation was extended
  to January 2027 and that B2B mandatory e-invoicing implementation work was
  still being completed during 2026.

contracting entity requires professional review.

## 6. Review rule

Market sources and prices change. Review this benchmark at least every six months
and before:

- publishing public prices;
- launching marketplace or Merchant of Record distribution;
- entering a new jurisdiction;
- offering a new SLA or support tier;
- signing an OEM, white-label, reseller, or public-sector agreement.
