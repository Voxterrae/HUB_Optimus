# HUB_Optimus — Revenue Ambition Model v0.1

**Status:** Management scenario proposed under issue `#1863`  
**Model date:** 8 August 2026  
**Currency:** EUR  
**Nature:** Bookings and ARR planning model, not a guarantee, valuation, tax
forecast, accounting statement, or public offer

## 1. Objective

The commercial objective is not to accumulate many low-value customers. It is to
secure a limited number of institutional relationships that pay for platform,
applications, connectors, usage, distribution, implementation, and support.

The initial operating target is:

> **EUR 5 million of gross contracted value in the first twelve months after
> commercial readiness, with at least EUR 2 million of exit annual recurring
> revenue.**

The target assumes three external institutional customers, controlled scope, paid
pilots, one material implementation, and productized recurring components.

This is deliberately ambitious. It requires productization, trusted delivery
partners, financial controls, secure multi-tenant operation, reference customers,
and refusal of underfunded work.

## 7. Capacity rule

Revenue is not accepted when delivery quality, security, liquidity, or owner
control would be compromised.

Before contracting each material deal, confirm:

- implementation and support capacity;
- cash received before mobilization;
- gross margin after channel and partner cost;
- tenant, data, security, and compliance readiness;
- customer dependencies and acceptance authority;
- liability and insurance;
- product reuse versus custom burden;
- impact on roadmap and existing customers;
- no transfer of protected HUB_Optimus core rights.

## 8. What Benjamin Gerrit Hoff personally receives

This model forecasts company-level commercial value, not personal take-home pay.
The amount ultimately available to Benjamin Gerrit Hoff depends on:

- contracting and IP-owning entity;
- founder salary or management fee;
- dividends and retained earnings;
- corporate and personal taxation;
- reinvestment, reserves, insurance, and working capital;
- shareholder and joint-venture agreements, if any.

No personal-net figure should be promised until legal, accounting, and tax
advisers design the structure. The commercial target is first to create clean,
auditable company revenue and preserve ownership and liquidity.

## 9. Commercial trigger

The operating target starts only when all of the following are ready:

1. verified contracting entity and bank account;
2. external accountant/tax adviser and standard invoice process;
3. standard MSA, order form, DPA, SLA, IP, and support schedules;
4. product SKUs and entitlement model;
5. billing and usage metering;
6. Microsoft Marketplace publisher path or approved transaction channel;
7. security and privacy package;
8. delivery and support capacity;
9. controlled public evidence and `Request Institutional Access` intake.
