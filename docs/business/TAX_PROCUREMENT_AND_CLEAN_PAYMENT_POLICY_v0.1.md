# HUB_Optimus — Tax, Procurement, and Clean Payment Policy v0.1

**Status:** Owner decision recorded in issue `#1863`; repository ratification proposed  
**Owner and final commercial authority:** Benjamin Gerrit Hoff  
**Principle:** The customer funds and manages its internal procurement and tax workflow; HUB_Optimus receives clear, lawful, traceable net payment

## 1. Purpose and legal reality

HUB_Optimus will not become an unpaid extension of a customer's accounting,
procurement, tax, legal, or vendor-management department.

The customer must use its own staff, advisers, budgets, systems, and internal
approvals to make a lawful purchase possible. Internal bureaucracy is not part of
the technology price unless explicitly quoted.

No contract can lawfully eliminate statutory duties that belong to the
HUB_Optimus contracting entity. HUB_Optimus must still maintain its own compliant
invoicing, accounting, tax, sanctions, data-protection, and corporate records.
The objective is to minimize operational distraction and allocate each duty to
the party that controls it—not to pretend legal duties do not exist.

## 2. Customer procurement pack

Before work or capacity reservation, the customer must provide:

- exact legal contracting name;
- registered address and country;
- company, public-body, or registry number;
- valid VAT or equivalent tax number;
- VIES validation where relevant;
- billing address and invoice language;
- purchase order or equivalent budget authorization;
- one commercial owner and one accounts-payable owner;
- invoicing portal, FACe, marketplace, or e-invoice instructions;
- tax-residency, exemption, withholding, or treaty documents where relevant;
- bank-remittance and payment-reference requirements;
- security, data-processing, accessibility, and public-procurement requirements;
- confirmation of the real beneficiary and source of funds.

The customer is responsible for obtaining internal approvals and ensuring that
its purchase order and invoicing instructions match the signed commercial terms.

## 3. No work before cleared commercial readiness

Work begins only after all three conditions exist:

```text
signed contract or binding order
        +
complete procurement and billing pack
        +
cleared initial payment
```

A letter of intent, internal approval email, procurement ticket, budget promise,
future grant, pending purchase order, or verbal instruction does not reserve
capacity unless accompanied by a paid reservation or mobilization agreement.

## 4. Vendor activation and procurement burden

For enterprise and public-sector prospects:

- vendor activation retainer: EUR 10,000;
- it is creditable against the first contracted engagement when contracting
  completes within 30 days;
- it is non-refundable when the customer abandons, delays, or materially changes
  its procurement after HUB_Optimus has performed onboarding work;
- standard setup includes two administrative hours;
- additional bespoke forms, portals, questionnaires, tax packs, legal redlines,
  meetings, or accounting coordination are EUR 500/hour, minimum EUR 5,000;
- bespoke RFP, tender, grant, or procurement solution design is EUR
  25,000–75,000.

HUB_Optimus may decline a process whose administrative burden, liability, payment
risk, or requested rights are disproportionate to the commercial value.

## 5. Payment channels

Approved channels are:

1. verified bank transfer to the contracting entity;
2. an approved Merchant of Record for digital subscriptions and standardized
   software sales;
3. an approved enterprise marketplace, including Microsoft Marketplace where
   appropriate;
4. another regulated channel expressly approved in writing.

Not accepted:

- cash;
- anonymous cryptocurrency;
- gift cards or informal substitutes;
- payments to personal accounts;
- unverified third-party payers;
- split payments designed to avoid controls;
- funds whose source, ownership, purpose, or sanctions status cannot be verified.

HUB_Optimus may pause onboarding, reject, or return suspicious funds and request
reasonable KYC, beneficial-owner, sanctions, or source-of-funds evidence.

## 6. Direct B2B, marketplace, and Merchant of Record

### Direct B2B

Preferred for strategic enterprise, public-sector, and implementation contracts.
The customer must provide the tax and procurement information needed for a
compliant invoice and must operate its internal accounts-payable process.

### Enterprise marketplace

A marketplace may simplify supplier onboarding and consolidate customer billing.
The marketplace fee, tax treatment, payout timing, refunds, withholding, and
channel risk must be priced into the offer so HUB_Optimus receives its required
net amount.

### Merchant of Record

An approved Merchant of Record may act as seller to the end customer and manage
subscription billing, payment methods, transaction taxes, invoices, fraud,
refunds, and chargebacks for eligible digital products.

A Merchant of Record reduces global transaction-tax operations; it does not
remove all corporate income-tax, accounting, payout, transfer-pricing,
withholding, permanent-establishment, or local legal questions for HUB_Optimus.

## 7. Taxes and gross-up

Unless the contract expressly states otherwise:

- prices exclude VAT, GST, sales, use, digital-services, withholding, customs,
  stamp, marketplace, and equivalent transaction taxes or duties;
- the customer pays taxes legally chargeable to it;
- HUB_Optimus remains responsible for taxes imposed on its own net income;
- bank, correspondent-bank, foreign-exchange, and intermediary fees are borne by
  the customer so the invoiced net amount is received;
- where a customer must legally withhold tax, the price is grossed up so
  HUB_Optimus receives the agreed net amount, to the extent legally permitted;
- the customer must provide an official withholding certificate promptly;
- unusual local registrations, local tax opinions, tax representation, tender
  guarantees, or permanent-establishment analysis are separately funded by the
  customer and require professional review.

No customer may unilaterally deduct tax, fees, penalties, procurement costs,
reseller margin, or bank charges without contractual authority.

## 8. EU VAT and customer validation

For EU B2B transactions, the customer's VAT status and number must be validated
where relevant. The invoice must apply the legally correct VAT treatment,
including reverse charge where applicable.

If the customer does not supply a valid VAT number or required evidence, HUB_Optimus
may charge the applicable VAT, delay activation, use an approved Merchant of
Record, or decline the transaction.

## 9. Public institutions

Public institutions are paid clients by default.

Before mobilization they must provide:

- lawful procurement route;
- budget reservation and responsible contracting authority;
- purchase order or equivalent commitment;
- electronic-invoice route and organizational codes;
- acceptance authority and timetable;
- payment deadline and legal basis;
- one person responsible for resolving procurement and invoice rejection.

HUB_Optimus does not finance a public body by starting work before a valid order.
A humanitarian emergency waiver requires an explicit written decision by
Benjamin Gerrit Hoff and remains subject to lawful authority and safeguarding.

## 10. Payment timing

- Annual platform, application, connector, embed, and support fees are prepaid.
- Usage overages and agreed pass-through consumption may be invoiced monthly in
  arrears.
- Diagnostics and workshops are normally prepaid.
- Paid pilots and implementations follow the milestone schedule in the proposal.
- Quotation validity is 15 calendar days unless stated otherwise.
- The customer's internal approval process does not alter a contractual due date.
- Delivery capacity is released when reservation or initial payment is not
  received by the deadline.

## 11. Late payment and suspension

Where legally permitted, overdue amounts trigger:

- the contractual or statutory late-payment interest;
- statutory fixed recovery compensation;
- reasonable additional recovery costs;
- suspension of access, support, deployment, or work after notice;
- loss of reserved capacity;
- reinstatement fees where operational work is required.

Suspension must protect data, security, and third parties, but HUB_Optimus is not
required to continue unfunded service.

## 12. No dependency on customer accounting departments

The customer must appoint one billing owner with authority to resolve invoice
rejection, portal errors, tax documents, purchase-order mismatch, and payment
status.

HUB_Optimus provides one compliant invoice and the agreed standard vendor pack.
It does not chase multiple departments, repeatedly re-enter unchanged data,
attend unpaid internal meetings, or redesign contractual terms to fit an
unfunded process.

Additional accounting-department work is billable procurement support.

## 13. Clean-money standard

Every payment must be:

- lawful;
- traceable;
- linked to the contracting entity and agreement;
- received through an approved channel;
- free of undisclosed beneficial ownership or prohibited sanctions exposure;
- supported by the required invoice and remittance evidence.

The objective is simple: clear contract, clear invoice, clear payer, clear tax
treatment, clear net receipt, and auditable records.

## 14. Common Good

Approved Common Good work remains free to the eligible beneficiary. It may still
use a grant, sponsor, or donor to cover unavoidable third-party costs at cost and
without HUB_Optimus margin.

The clean-money, safeguarding, data, sanctions, and beneficiary-integrity controls
still apply. A charitable label never excuses unclear funds or a hidden
commercial beneficiary.
