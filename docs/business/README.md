## Channel decision

```text
Microsoft-aligned enterprise software/private offer
    → Microsoft Marketplace

AWS-centered professional services or multi-cloud buyer
    → AWS Marketplace

Standardized low-touch international subscription
    → approved Merchant of Record

Strategic, regulated, sovereign, unusual, or multi-million transformation
    → direct B2B or negotiated private marketplace offer
```

The selected channel does not reduce the target HUB_Optimus net price. Marketplace,
Merchant of Record, reseller, banking, foreign-exchange, withholding, and
procurement costs are incorporated into the customer price where lawful.
