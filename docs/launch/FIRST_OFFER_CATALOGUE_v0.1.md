# HUB_Optimus — First Institutional Offer Catalogue v0.1

**Status:** Draft launch catalogue under issue `#1867`  
**Owner and final commercial authority:** Benjamin Gerrit Hoff

## Offer A — Institutional Diagnostic

### Purpose

Create a decision-grade architecture and commercial roadmap before any material
implementation.

### Price

- Standard institutional range: EUR 60,000–120,000 excluding tax.
- Enterprise/public/regulated complexity may require a higher quotation under the
  controlling v0.2 price book.
- Paid before work according to the signed order; no free custom pre-analysis.

### Included

- executive problem and beneficiary definition;
- current systems, data, workflow, identity, and dependency map;
- operational-risk and evidence model;
- target HUB_Optimus architecture;
- application, connector, API, environment, and entitlement map;
- security, audit, rollback, support, and exit boundaries;
- readiness and gap assessment;
- staged implementation and Proof-of-Value plan;
- acceptance criteria;
- commercial SKU and rights proposal.

### Excluded

- production deployment;
- unrestricted source code;
- ownership, co-ownership, or control;
- white-label, OEM, resale, sublicensing, or exclusivity;
- unlimited meetings, tender work, procurement administration, or legal redlines;
- third-party licenses, cloud, travel, or infrastructure;
- formal legal, tax, accounting, or regulatory opinion.

## Offer B — Governed Proof of Value

### Purpose

Prove one high-value institutional outcome under controlled data, identity,
audit, and rollback conditions.

### Price

- EUR 250,000–500,000 excluding tax for the initial target scope.
- Final price depends on risk, data, systems, integrations, rights, environment,
  support, and procurement burden.

### Included

- one operating domain or workflow;
- governed tenant and identity scope;
- agreed applications/connectors/API surfaces;
- measurable baseline and acceptance criteria;
- audit trail and rollback;
- limited training and handoff;
- security and productization review;
- recurring platform and implementation proposal.

### Excluded

- free custom proof of concept;
- broad enterprise rollout;
- unrelated integrations;
- source-code transfer;
- perpetual or unlimited rights;
- production SLA unless expressly contracted;
- rights for affiliates, downstream customers, or resellers not named in the
  order.

## Offer C — Enterprise Implementation

### Purpose

Deploy and operationalize the approved architecture after a successful diagnostic
or Proof of Value.

### Initial target

- EUR 750,000–1,500,000 for the first anchor implementation;
- broader enterprise and public-sector programs follow the v0.2 range of
  EUR 750,000–3,000,000+;
- strategic transformation may exceed these ranges.

### Separate recurring components

- annual HUB_Optimus platform canon;
- applications and modules;
- connector canon;
- API/HUB Units and third-party consumption;
- embedded/iframe/SDK rights;
- environments and dedicated infrastructure;
- support and SLA;
- OEM, white-label, resale, exclusivity, or downstream tenant rights.

## Qualification statement

A prospect can evaluate public evidence and attend one 30-minute qualification
conversation. Bespoke diagnosis, architecture, demonstration, prototype,
integration design, tender response, or reusable output requires a paid
engagement.
