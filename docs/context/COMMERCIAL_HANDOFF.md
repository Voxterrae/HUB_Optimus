## Controlling commercial posture

HUB_Optimus is selective and inbound-first.

It does not chase companies, public institutions, integrators, or large
organizations by delivering free bespoke work. Suitable prospects must identify
the legal entity, real beneficiary, decision authority, budget, procurement
route, systems, data boundary, and requested rights.

One introductory qualification conversation may be free once for up to 30
minutes. It includes no custom diagnosis, architecture, code, report, prototype,
tender design, integration plan, or reusable deliverable.

Commercial and public-sector proofs of concept are paid.

## What is monetized separately

A complete agreement may contain:

1. diagnostic, architecture, pilot, and implementation;
2. annual HUB_Optimus platform canon;
3. application and module licenses;
4. connector activation and annual connector canon;
5. API request packages and HUB Unit consumption;
6. embedded application, iframe, SDK, white-label, OEM, or resale rights;
7. users, environments, cloud consumption, support, SLA, and dedicated operation.

Payment for one component does not grant the others. Implementation does not
replace the annual canon. BYOK does not remove orchestration and governance
charges. Embedding does not grant ownership. White-label, resale, source-code,
exclusivity, and OEM rights require separate agreements.

## Procurement, tax information, and clean payment

HUB_Optimus does not become an unpaid extension of a customer's procurement,
accounting, tax, legal, or vendor-management department.

Work begins only after:

```text
signed contract or binding order
        +
complete procurement and billing pack
        +
cleared initial payment
```

The customer must provide its legal identity, registration and VAT/tax number,
purchase order or budget authority, billing/e-invoice instructions, tax or
withholding documents, one commercial owner, one accounts-payable owner, and
the real beneficiary and source of funds.

Enterprise and public-sector vendor activation carries a EUR 10,000 retainer.
Two administrative hours are included. Additional bespoke procurement,
accounting, portal, tax-form, or legal coordination is EUR 500/hour with a
EUR 5,000 minimum.

HUB_Optimus may sell through:

- verified direct bank transfer;
- an approved Merchant of Record for eligible standardized digital sales;
- an approved enterprise marketplace;
- another regulated owner-approved channel.

Prices preserve the required HUB_Optimus net amount after marketplace, Merchant
of Record, reseller, bank, foreign-exchange, withholding, and intermediary
costs, to the extent legally permitted.

The customer manages and funds its internal purchasing bureaucracy. HUB_Optimus
still complies with its own non-transferable invoicing, accounting, tax, legal,
sanctions, and data-protection duties. No commercial policy may falsely claim
that statutory duties disappear.

## Clean-money boundary

Accept only lawful, traceable funds linked to the contracting entity, agreement,
invoice, and approved payment channel.

Do not accept cash, anonymous cryptocurrency, gift cards, personal-account
payments, unverified third-party payers, or payment splitting intended to evade
controls. KYC, beneficial-owner, sanctions, and source-of-funds evidence may be
required.

## Common Good

Qualifying human-welfare, animal-welfare, humanitarian, essential-rights, and
harm-prevention nonprofit work may be free to the approved beneficiary.

Do not monetize suffering, donations, vulnerability, private stories, images,
health information, rescue volume, or beneficiary data. Pro bono scope remains
limited, noncommercial, nontransferable, and does not transfer the HUB_Optimus
core or create unlimited support.

Public institutions remain paid clients by default. A humanitarian emergency
waiver requires an explicit written decision by Benjamin Gerrit Hoff.
