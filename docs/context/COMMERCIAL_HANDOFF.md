# HUB_Optimus Commercial Handoff

**Owner decision record:** issue `#1863`  
**Owner and final commercial authority:** Benjamin Gerrit Hoff

## Controlling rule

Companies, commercial organizations, funded institutions, public bodies, and
public institutions pay for bespoke HUB_Optimus professional work.

A free qualification conversation is limited to one call of up to 30 minutes and
contains no bespoke analysis, architecture, code, report, prototype, tender
response, or reusable deliverable.

Proofs of concept for commercial and public-sector clients are paid.

## Positioning

HUB_Optimus is positioned as founder-led architecture, decision intelligence,
evidence governance, enterprise operating-system design, governed integration,
security, auditability, and managed capability. It is not generic free IT help.

Credibility comes from working systems, reproducible checks, controlled pilots,
measurable results, and documented boundaries—not from donating custom delivery
to organizations that can pay.

## Current starting prices

- SME diagnostic: EUR 3,000–7,500.
- Enterprise/public diagnostic: EUR 10,000–25,000.
- SME paid pilot: EUR 15,000–45,000.
- Enterprise/public paid pilot: EUR 50,000–150,000.
- SME implementation: EUR 30,000–120,000.
- Enterprise/public implementation: EUR 120,000–500,000+.
- Recurring managed capability: EUR 2,500–10,000/month SME and EUR
  10,000–50,000+/month enterprise/public.
- Founder architecture: EUR 2,500/day.
- Engineering/integration: EUR 1,500/day.
- Urgent work: 1.5x.
- Minimum engagement: EUR 3,000 SME; EUR 10,000 enterprise/public.

Exact quotations depend on scope and contract. Do not present the bottom of a
range as the default.

## Common Good rule

Qualifying human-welfare, animal-welfare, humanitarian, essential-rights, and
harm-prevention nonprofit work may be free to the beneficiary through
HUB_Optimus Common Good.

Do not monetize suffering. Do not take a percentage of donations. Do not sell or
reuse beneficiary data, vulnerability, private stories, images, health, rescue
volume, or emergencies. Do not require publicity as payment.

Unavoidable external costs may be covered at cost without HUB_Optimus margin.
Pro bono scope is limited, noncommercial, nontransferable, and does not transfer
the HUB_Optimus core or create unlimited support.

Public institutions remain paid clients by default. A humanitarian emergency
waiver requires an explicit written decision by Benjamin Gerrit Hoff.

## Exception rule

No AI, employee, contributor, collaborator, reseller, prospect, or public
official may create a pricing, free-work, IP, joint-venture, or Common Good
exception by implication.

Exceptions require a written owner decision identifying scope, duration,
consideration, risks, and whether precedent exists.
