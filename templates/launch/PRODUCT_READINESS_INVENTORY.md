## Readiness definitions

- **Concept:** documented idea without working evidence.
- **Prototype:** working demonstration with material manual, security, reliability,
  or support gaps.
- **Tested:** repeatable evidence exists for the defined test boundary.
- **Pilot-ready:** safe for a controlled paid customer pilot with explicit limits,
  support, audit, and rollback.
- **Production-ready:** approved for the contracted production boundary, including
  security, operations, support, continuity, and exit.

No higher readiness state may be assigned solely from narrative or file presence.
